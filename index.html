        Questions

        Which glasses does it work with?

        Network&Recall is being designed for Meta AI glasses, with a companion phone app for reviewing contacts and drafts. Because Live lookup speaks to you through the open-ear speakers instead of a display, it works on Meta AI glasses with or without a screen. When you join, tell us whether you own a pair. It helps us decide what to support first.

        Does it record the people I talk to?

        In Live recall, the conversation is turned into a short text summary and the audio is discarded. No voice recordings and no face data are stored. If you'd rather not capture live at all, use Quick recap and dictate a few sentences after you walk away.

        What does Live lookup tell me, and where does it come from?

        When you look at a name badge, the glasses read the name and organization, then quietly speak a short brief through the open-ear speakers: current role, research or work areas, and recent public work. It draws only on public professional sources like company pages and publication listings, plays at a low volume meant for you, and isn't saved unless you keep the contact. It never uses faces, you can turn it off anytime, and anyone can opt out of appearing in lookups.

        Is it okay to capture conversations at events?

        Recording rules vary by place, and some require everyone's consent. That's why Live recall shows a visible capture light, why we suggest asking “Mind if I take notes?”, and why Quick recap exists for situations where live capture isn't appropriate.

        What does pre-ordering mean?

        It reserves founding-member pricing for you. No payment is collected now. We'll email you to confirm before launch, and you can cancel anytime.

        Who qualifies for student and researcher pricing?

        Students, postdocs, and researchers at universities and research institutes. You'll verify with a .edu or institutional email when you sign up.

        Can I get my contacts out?

        Yes. Export any event to a spreadsheet, or copy a follow-up draft into your own email. Try Export in the app preview at the top of this page.

        Is this a real product yet?

        Not yet. Network&Recall is an early-stage concept developed in ECE 6001 at Georgia Tech. Sign-ups tell us whether, and for whom, it's worth building.
